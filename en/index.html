The honest method

Solid evidence vs. speculation

The 2028 warning mixes real trends with numbers from a single video or article. This course teaches you to distinguish them—and marks every figure or model version repeated from the video as (according to the video—unverified).

✓ More solid

Categories and trends supported by broad evidence, not dependent on a single source.

  • ✓The task-horizon trend from METR (the curve rises over time).
  • ✓The MirrorCode concept: rebuilding software in the dark as a measure of what AI can do alone.
  • ✓The measurement instability when a model cheats (cheating / reward hacking).
  • ✓Anthropic’s public statement that most of its code is written by Claude.
  • ✓RSI as a serious topic raised by Clark (Anthropic), Hassabis (DeepMind), and Hinton.

⚠ Treat as claims to verify

Exact numbers and names from one video or article—useful as illustrations, not established facts.

  • ⚠Exact model names and versions ("Opus 4.7", "GPT-5.6 Sol", "Claude 3 Opus 52×").
  • ⚠The "~60% by 2028" —one person’s estimate, not a field-wide consensus.
  • ⚠Exact figures repeated from the video: 56%, 19 days, US$2,600, US$200M.
  • ⚠Any claim that the curve "proves" a predetermined outcome—extrapolating a straight line is no guarantee.

Course rule: whenever we cite a model version or figure from the video, we mark it (according to the video—unverified).
